# Build a Taiwan semiconductor supply-chain introduction page

You are building an educational introduction for readers interested in Taiwan semiconductor stocks. Use Traditional Chinese (Taiwan) for the page. Explain company roles without implying that every company is a direct supplier to every other company. Do not give a buy/sell recommendation, price target, live quote or unsupported relationship.

Build three assets using `write_asset`: `index.html`, `styles.css`, and `app.js`. The page must be responsive, keyboard accessible, and useful without JavaScript. It must show the five representative roles and tickers from the supplied source data, source links, and a clear statement that the map is illustrative. Use semantic HTML, focus styles, and reduced-motion handling. Keep external scripts, remote fonts and third-party trackers out of the output.

Your workspace is a candidate artifact, not the published site. The runtime validates and holds it at an owner gate. You cannot approve or publish your own work.
